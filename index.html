About

Hi, I'm Yichi — I study when multimodal and embodied models break, and how to measure it.

I am an undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Technology (Project 211 & Double First-Class), and a research intern at HKUST (Guangzhou) advised by Prof. Renjing Xu. My work sits where trustworthy multimodal AI meets embodied intelligence — probing how vision-, language-, and action-models fail under adversarial and physical stress, and building benchmarks that make those failures measurable. So far I've contributed to four published / accepted papers and two works under review, including a Best Student Paper Award at the IJCAI 2025 workshop.

INTERESTS  ·  Embodied AI  ·  Explainable AI (XAI)  ·  Model Compression  ·  AI for Security
